Frequently Asked

How is this different from a tweak pack I download myself?

Every HEXA order is done 1-on-1, live, based on your actual hardware and how you use your system. There's no generic .bat file — settings are chosen specifically for your setup.

Where does the actual work happen?

Everything is coordinated through the HEXA Discord. Submitting the form below gets your request into a queue and opens the door to a ticket — payment and delivery both happen there.

Will this void my warranty or overclock my hardware?

No. BIOS tweaks included in PRIME do not touch CPU or RAM overclocking — they're limited to safe, non-destructive settings.

Do I need to be present during the optimization?

Yes — it's a live, 1-on-1 session over Discord so settings can be tailored to you in real time and you can ask questions as they come up.
